Gemma Ong

Board of Directors/Officer

Gemma Ong is the President of PCCI Valenzuela (2026-2027). A Mapua-trained industrial engineer (Batch '89), she owns Food Grade Trading and founded the Blue Dragon Cooking Oil brand. She now leads the chamber in serving over 20,000 local enterprises through V-Link and advocating for business-friendly policies in Valenzuela City.
